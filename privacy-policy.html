Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sketch Art AI, operated by Playtech Studio, handles information when you use this website.

1. Information you provide

When you use the sketch tool, you choose to upload a photo for processing. The website sends the selected image to its image-processing service so that sketch previews and, after successful payment, HD sketches can be generated. Please upload only images you have the right to use.

2. Image processing

Image processing is handled through our Cloudflare Worker API and Cloudflare Workers AI service. The website uses the uploaded image to provide the requested sketch-generation feature. Avoid uploading confidential or sensitive images. We do not ask you to upload identity documents or payment-card details to the sketch tool.

3. Payments

The optional HD unlock is priced at ₹99 and is processed through Razorpay. Payment information is handled by Razorpay under its own privacy and security terms. Sketch Art AI does not collect or store your full card number, UPI PIN, or banking password.

4. Advertising and cookies

This website may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners. Those partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertisements, subject to their own policies and your applicable settings. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website or advertising features.

Google may use data collected through its advertising services as described in Google's advertising policies. You can review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

The website relies on third-party services, including Cloudflare for image processing, Razorpay for payments, and advertising providers when ads are enabled. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to use the services needed to operate the website. Internet transmission and online storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information may be retained by service providers according to their systems, settings, and legal obligations.

7. Children's privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact
